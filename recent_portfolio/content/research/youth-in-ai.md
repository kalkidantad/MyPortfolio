The project solves the most critical problem: lack of reliable data.

My contribution includes the explainability and prediction API by building a FastAPI service layer with SHAP/XGBoost integration, the forecast fusion layer, and real-time data ingestion pipelines from sources such as CHIRPS, GloFAS and Flood Hub; showing how much each source pushed the final score above or below a neutral baseline, which makes multi-source forecasts auditable and actionable.
